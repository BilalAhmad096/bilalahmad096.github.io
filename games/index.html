Grid Balancer

Run a power grid for a day. Demand rises and falls, the wind and the sun come and go, and every moment the power going in has to match the power coming out. When it doesn't, the frequency drifts away from 50 Hz. Keep it close and keep the lights on until midnight.

    How to play
    • A whole day runs in three minutes. Score comes from every second the frequency stays close to 50 Hz, and is highest within 0.05 Hz of it.
    • The gap between the two dashed lines on the chart is roughly what gas and hydro will need to cover. Gas ramps slowly, so move it before that gap moves.
    • Use pumped hydro for the quick corrections, and pump water back up when there is spare power.
    • Past 49.5 or 50.5 Hz, protection acts on its own: it cuts customers off or trips generators, and costs you points. If it has to act three times, or the frequency reaches 49 or 51 Hz, the grid blacks out.
    • Gas emissions are taken off your score at the end, so do not run more than you need.
    • Keys: Q/A gas up and down, W/S hydro, E peaker, P pause.

    This is a game, much simpler than the real grid, but the ideas are the real ones: Great Britain's grid runs at 50 Hz and must stay between 49.8 and 50.2 Hz in normal operation, and its control room really does plan for the surge of kettles at half-time.
