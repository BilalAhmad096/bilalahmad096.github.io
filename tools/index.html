    Anthropic (ANT) IPO: not Shariah compliant

    Not halal under AAOIFI or Mufti Taqi Usmani's conditions
    Nasdaq: ANT · price range $70–$78 · screened on the S-1 balance sheet at 30 June 2026

    Anthropic's business, building AI models and software, is permissible. The shares fail because of what the company holds and earns: most of its assets are cash and interest-bearing investments, and its interest income is above the 5% limit both standards allow.

    AAOIFI (Shariah Standard No. 21): fails one of four tests

    • ✗Fails: Interest income: $1.11B in the first half of 2026, 6.8% of $16.3B revenue (limit 5%).
    • ✓Passes: Business activity: AI models and software.
    • ✓Passes: Interest-bearing debt: Amazon convertible notes of $6.2B principal, about 0.6% of a roughly $1T market value (limit 30%).
    • ✓Passes: Interest-bearing cash and investments: $131.7B, about 13% of market value (limit 30%).

    Mufti Taqi Usmani (KMI-30 thresholds): fails three of six tests

    • ✗Fails: Illiquid assets: about 11% of total assets (at least 25% needed). $131.7B of $147.4B is cash and short-term investments.
    • ✗Fails: Interest-bearing cash and investments: about 89% of total assets (limit 33%).
    • ✗Fails: Interest income: 6.8% of revenue (limit 5%).
    • ✓Passes: Business activity: AI models and software.
    • ✓Passes: Interest-bearing debt: $6.2B principal, about 4% of total assets (limit 37%).
    • ✓Passes: Share price above the net liquid assets each share represents.

    The IPO itself would raise a further $64–74B in cash, so the liquidity tests would move further past their limits, not back within them. Figures are from the S-1 as reported by analysts of the filing; market value is taken at the top of the price range (about $1T on shares outstanding). This is general information on two published standards, not a fatwa or investment advice. Updated 15 November 2026.

    Anthropic (ANT) debut: highly likely to open above its IPO price

    Estimate: about an 85% chance the first trade is above the offer price
    Expected first trade: Thursday 19 November 2026, around 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. New York time (4:30 to 6:30 p.m. UK)

    Anthropic prices its shares on the evening of 18 November and starts trading on Nasdaq the next day. A huge IPO opens only once the exchange has matched the orders waiting for it, usually around midday rather than at the 9:30 a.m. bell; SpaceX's first trade this June came at 11:46 a.m. Everything we can see points to strong opening demand, though not to an unlimited one.

    The estimate

    • •Opens above the final IPO price: about 85%.
    • •Opens at least 10% above it: about 60%.
    • •Most likely opening range: 10% to 25% above the final IPO price.
    • •Closes its first day above the IPO price: about 80%.

    Why it is likely

    • ▲For: Demand: the order book closed around six to seven times covered, and the price is expected at or above the top of the $70–$78 range.
    • ▲For: Pre-IPO markets: contracts tracking ANT have traded 20% to 30% above the range in recent days.
    • ▲For: Comparable giants: SpaceX (June 2026) opened 11% above its $135 offer and closed up 19%; Alibaba opened about 36% higher; Arm and Saudi Aramco about 10% higher. Each of them opened above its offer.
    • ▲For: Base rate: about three in four US IPOs since 1980 have closed their first day above the offer price, and heavily oversubscribed deals do better than that.
    • ▲For: Support: the underwriters can buy shares at the offer price in the first days, which makes a fall below it at the open less likely.

    What could stop it

    • ▼Against: A higher price: pricing above the range takes some of the first-day gain into the offer itself.
    • ▼Against: Size: at $64–74B this would be among the largest IPOs ever, and a very large float leaves less unmet demand at the open.
    • ▼Against: Over-optimism: pre-IPO contracts overstated SpaceX's opening price by about 17%.
    • ▼Against: The market on the day, and the risk of a technical delay, as at Facebook's 2012 debut.

    This is an estimate built from public reports as of 18 November 2026, before the final price was set; it will be updated once ANT trades. It is not investment advice, and the screen above finds the shares not Shariah compliant.
