    IPO Shariah screens

    Anthropic (ANT) IPO: not Shariah compliant

    Not halal under AAOIFI or Mufti Taqi Usmani's conditions
    Nasdaq: ANT · price range $70–$78 · screened on the S-1 balance sheet at 30 June 2026

    Anthropic's business, building AI models and software, is permissible. The shares fail because of what the company holds and earns: most of its assets are cash and interest-bearing investments, and its interest income is above the 5% limit both standards allow.

    AAOIFI (Shariah Standard No. 21): fails one of four tests

    • ✗Fails: Interest income: $1.11B in the first half of 2026, 6.8% of $16.3B revenue (limit 5%).
    • ✓Passes: Business activity: AI models and software.
    • ✓Passes: Interest-bearing debt: Amazon convertible notes of $6.2B principal, about 0.6% of a roughly $1T market value (limit 30%).
    • ✓Passes: Interest-bearing cash and investments: $131.7B, about 13% of market value (limit 30%).

    Mufti Taqi Usmani (KMI-30 thresholds): fails three of six tests

    • ✗Fails: Illiquid assets: about 11% of total assets (at least 25% needed). $131.7B of $147.4B is cash and short-term investments.
    • ✗Fails: Interest-bearing cash and investments: about 89% of total assets (limit 33%).
    • ✗Fails: Interest income: 6.8% of revenue (limit 5%).
    • ✓Passes: Business activity: AI models and software.
    • ✓Passes: Interest-bearing debt: $6.2B principal, about 4% of total assets (limit 37%).
    • ✓Passes: Share price above the net liquid assets each share represents.

    The IPO itself would raise a further $64–74B in cash, so the liquidity tests would move further past their limits, not back within them. Figures are from the S-1 as reported by analysts of the filing; market value is taken at the top of the price range (about $1T on shares outstanding). This is general information on two published standards, not a fatwa or investment advice. Updated 15 November 2026.
